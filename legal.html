Privacy and terms

Privacy policy

VidaClip edits, renders, and transcribes your media on your computer. VidaClip does not collect or upload your local video files, audio tracks, or personal recordings. The browser extension sends the links and clip selections you submit to the Desktop app on the same computer.

VidaClip connects to video platforms when you fetch linked media, to GitHub for updates, and to model hosts to download optional processing models. If you connect an assistant using VidaClip Automation, see that assistant’s privacy settings for how it handles prompts and tool results.

Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and billing. VidaClip does not receive or store your credit card information. Desktop contacts the license service when you activate a paid plan.
